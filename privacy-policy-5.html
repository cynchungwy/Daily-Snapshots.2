4. HOW WE COLLECT YOUR DATA

4.1 Data You Provide Directly

We collect data you actively provide when you register for an account, capture and upload photos or journal entries, make a purchase or subscription, contact us for support, or provide feedback.

4.2 Data Collected Automatically

When you use the Application, we and our service providers automatically collect certain technical data including device identifiers, IP address, OS version, app version, crash reports, and usage patterns via our infrastructure partners (Base44, Supabase, MongoDB).

4.3 Data From Third-Party Providers

We receive limited data from RevenueCat (subscription status, transaction IDs) and Apple (App Store purchase verification). We do not receive raw payment card details — these are handled entirely by RevenueCat and Apple.

10. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher;
  • Encryption of data at rest where supported by our infrastructure providers;
  • Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms (we do not store plaintext passwords);
  • Access controls limiting data access to what is necessary for operation; and
  • Use of secure, reputable cloud infrastructure providers (Base44/Wix, Supabase, MongoDB).

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at cchungyn@gmail.com.

10.1 Data Breach Response

In the event of a personal data breach, we will: contain the breach promptly; notify the Hong Kong PCPD where required under the PDPO; notify affected EU/EEA users and supervisory authorities within 72 hours where required under GDPR Article 33; and notify affected users without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

13. COOKIES, TRACKING, AND DO-NOT-TRACK

13.1 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Prior to placing any non-essential cookies on your device through our web interface, we will present you with a cookie consent notice allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You may change your preferences at any time via the cookie settings link in our web interface footer.

  • Essential / strictly necessary: Required for the Application to function (e.g. session authentication tokens). Placed without requiring consent as they are necessary for core functionality and cannot be disabled.
  • Performance / analytics: Anonymised data collection to understand usage patterns. Requires your consent via the cookie consent notice.
  • Advertising (AdMob): If advertising features are enabled, Google AdMob may use device identifiers to serve contextual advertisements. You may opt out through iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

13.2 App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

In compliance with Apple's ATT framework, we will request your explicit permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You may grant or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

13.3 Do-Not-Track Signals

Our web interface does not currently alter its data collection or use practices in response to Do-Not-Track (DNT) signals from browsers. For the iOS Application, we honour your iOS privacy settings including Limit Ad Tracking and App Tracking Transparency. We do not engage in cross-app tracking without your explicit ATT consent.

14. PUSH NOTIFICATIONS

We use OneSignal to deliver push notifications. Push notifications are optional — you will be asked to consent when you first use the Application. You may withdraw consent at any time by disabling notifications for Daily Snapshots in your device Settings > Notifications, or by contacting us at cchungyn@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect your ability to use the core features of the Application.

15. PERSISTENT DEVICE IDENTIFIER

The Application uses iCloud Key-Value Storage (iOS) to store a persistent device identifier used exclusively for fraud prevention and abuse detection — specifically to prevent circumvention of free trial limitations and multi-account creation.

This identifier is not linked to your name or email address, is not shared with advertising networks, and is not used for tracking or profiling. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in protecting the commercial integrity of the Application.

If you wish to request deletion or reset of this identifier, please contact us at cchungyn@gmail.com. We will assess such requests individually, balancing your rights against our fraud prevention interests.
